# Interview preparation

Locate the exact job key and submitted PDF hash, archived posting, application
answers, and latest outcome. Do not assume the newest master was the submitted
version. Confirm the actual application date from receipts rather than folder names.

Ask which stage, date, format, interviewer role, and topics are known. Prepare:
- A concise introduction tied to actual experience and this role's responsibilities.
- A requirement-to-evidence map and candid gaps.
- Real project stories: question, method, candidate contribution, validation,
  result, limitations, and likely follow-ups. Unknown methods remain questions.
- Recruiter topics: interest, availability, location, compensation, and truthful
  authorization/sponsorship answers only in the appropriate context.
- Technical practice areas and examples matching the posting.
- Questions about team scope, success measures, process, and next steps.

Research the employer's current process and reported interview experiences when
requested. Prefer official sources for process claims. Label third-party anecdotes
with source and date; they are examples, not a guaranteed question bank. Avoid
confusing teams, levels, countries, or company products. Link claims to evidence.

Save the prep privately or in the user's specified Notion destination. Follow
the assessment's rules: prepare and practice, but do not impersonate the candidate
or provide prohibited assistance during an independent interview or assessment.
Track recruiter interest separately from offer probability and rejection reasons.
