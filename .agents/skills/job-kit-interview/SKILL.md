# Prepare for an interview

Resolve the kit root three directories above this skill. Read `AGENTS.md`, the
selected private profile, and [interview guide](../../../docs/interviews.md).
Locate the job's receipt, archived posting, and submitted PDF hash. Do not
substitute the latest resume or infer the application date from a folder name.

Confirm the stage and known format. Map requirements to verified examples, draft
an introduction and recruiter talking points, identify technical practice areas,
and prepare thoughtful team questions. Unknown methods or outcomes remain unknown.

Research current employer guidance and dated interview reports when requested.
Label anecdotes and uncertainty; do not promise specific questions. Save the
prep to the private workspace or the user's selected tracker destination.
Follow independent-assessment rules and do not impersonate the candidate.
